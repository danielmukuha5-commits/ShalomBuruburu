Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Shalom Hospital Buruburu.

Where is Shalom Hospital Buruburu located? +

Shalom Hospital Buruburu is located in Buruburu Phase 5, next to Kenol Petrol Station, along Mumias South Road, Nairobi. It is easily accessible from Umoja, Donholm, Maringo, and central Nairobi by matatu or boda boda.

Is Shalom Hospital open 24 hours? +

Yes. Shalom Hospital Buruburu is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including public holidays. Emergency services are available at all times. No appointment is necessary — walk in at any time.

How much is a gynaecologist consultation at Shalom Hospital? +

A Gynaecologist or Fertility Specialist consultation at Shalom Hospital Buruburu costs KSh 2,000. This includes an initial examination and treatment plan. To book, WhatsApp 0729 592676.

Does Shalom Hospital offer fertility treatment? +

Yes. Shalom Hospital Buruburu specialises in Reproductive Health and Fertility. Our Gynaecology and Fertility clinic offers fertility assessments, hormonal evaluations, HSG X-ray, ovulation monitoring, and treatment for PCOS, endometriosis, and unexplained infertility.

How much is a general consultation? +

A General Physician consultation costs KSh 500. Walk-ins are welcome 24 hours a day — no appointment needed.

Does Shalom Hospital accept SHA / SHIF insurance? +

Yes. Shalom Hospital Buruburu accepts SHA (Social Health Authority) and SHIF insurance. Please bring your SHA card when visiting. M-Pesa and cash payments are also accepted.

What is HSG X-ray and do you offer it? +

HSG (Hysterosalpingography) is an X-ray procedure that checks whether the fallopian tubes are open — a key fertility investigation. Yes, Shalom Hospital Buruburu offers HSG X-ray as part of our reproductive health and fertility services.

How do I book an appointment? +

The fastest way to book is via WhatsApp at 0729 592676. You can also call directly on 0737851959 or 0716 663290, or walk in at any time — no appointment is required for general consultation or emergency care.
