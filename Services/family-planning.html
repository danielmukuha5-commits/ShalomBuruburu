About This Service

Confidential Family Planning Care

Shalom Hospital Buruburu provides comprehensive, non-judgmental family planning services for all women. Whether you are choosing contraception for the first time, switching methods, seeking emergency contraception or having an implant or IUD removed — our qualified team provides expert counselling and the full range of options in a safe, private setting.

What We Treat

Family Planning Services We Offer

Contraceptive Counselling
One-to-one counselling to choose the right contraceptive method for your health, fertility plans and lifestyle.
Oral Contraceptive Pills
Combined and progestogen-only pills prescribed and dispensed — with clear instructions on timing and use.
Injectable Contraception (Depo-Provera)
Depo-Provera injection — 3-month injectable contraception administered by our qualified clinical team.
Contraceptive Implant (Implanon)
Single-rod implant insertion — providing up to 3 years of highly effective contraception, inserted under local anaesthetic.
Intrauterine Device (IUD)
Copper IUD (up to 10 years) and hormonal IUD insertion by our trained team — one of the most effective long-term contraceptive methods.
IUD & Implant Removal
Safe removal of existing IUDs and contraceptive implants — whether due to duration, side effects or pregnancy planning.
Emergency Contraception
Emergency contraceptive pills dispensed discreetly — most effective within 72 hours of unprotected intercourse.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Call us on 0737851959 or send us a WhatsApp — we're available 24 hours.

Is family planning confidential?
Absolutely. All family planning consultations and procedures at Shalom Hospital Buruburu are completely confidential and non-judgmental.
Which contraceptive method is best for me?
The best method depends on your health, age and fertility plans. Our clinical team provides personalised counselling to help you choose.
Can I get an IUD inserted at Shalom Hospital?
Yes. Both copper and hormonal IUD insertion are available. The procedure takes approximately 5–10 minutes and is performed under sterile conditions.
How soon after an implant or IUD can I have sex?
The copper IUD is effective immediately. The hormonal IUD and implant are effective within 7 days if inserted outside the first 5 days of your cycle.
